# Energy Balance Export — LEU Determination and DoE Template

## 1. Purpose

This document provides:
1. Determination of whether site-002 qualifies as a **Large Energy User (LEU)** under the National Energy Act 34/2008
2. An **energy balance export template** aligned to Department of Energy (DoE) requirements for LEU energy balance reporting
3. Guidance on how SENTINEL data feeds the export

**Reference:** National Energy Act 34/2008; DoE Large Energy User registration requirements.
**Owner:** Energy Lead / Facilities Manager.
**Review period:** Annually at start of DoE reporting cycle (typically June).

---

## 2. LEU Determination — Site-002

### 2.1 LEU Threshold Definition

A **Large Energy User (LEU)** is defined as a site consuming **>400 kWh per month** (approximately 13 MWh/month or 157 MWh/year).

Source: DoE Energy Efficiency Advisory: "Large Energy User" classification per National Energy Act s8 guidelines.

### 2.2 Site-002 Consumption Data

SENTINEL energy consumption history for site-002 (Sandton City Office Tower):

| Month | Total kWh | LEU Threshold (kWh) | Status |
|-------|-----------|---------------------|--------|
| 2026-04 | 23,262 | 400,000 | ❌ Below threshold |
| 2026-05 (partial) | 23,625 | 400,000 | ❌ Below threshold |

**Note:** May 2026 is a partial month (data up to 2026-05-19). Extrapolating to full month:
- May 2026 estimated full month: ~23,625 × (31/19) = **~38,600 kWh** — still below 400,000 kWh threshold.

### 2.3 LEU Determination Result

**Site-002 is NOT currently a Large Energy User.**

Threshold check: 23,262 kWh/month is ~5.8% of the 400,000 kWh/month LEU threshold.

> **Important caveat:** The LEU threshold may also apply at the **legal entity** level if Asikhwele Building Projects (Pty) Ltd operates multiple sites whose combined consumption exceeds 400,000 kWh/month. If additional sites are onboarded, recalculate combined entity consumption.

## 3. Implications

### If not an LEU:

- No mandatory DoE energy balance reporting obligation currently applies to site-002
- SENTINEL is still required to maintain energy metering data (good governance)
- FNB Supplier Registration (FSR) may still request energy efficiency evidence — this document serves that purpose

### If later classified as LEU (>400,000 kWh/month):

- Must register with DoE as LEU within 30 days of crossing threshold
- Submit annual energy balance report to DoE (template below)
- Required to implement an Energy Management System (EnMS) per SANS 50001 guidelines
- Maximum fine for non-compliance: R5 million or 2 years imprisonment (s43 of National Energy Act)

## 6. SENTINEL Export Query Reference

When preparing the DoE energy balance, use these Supabase queries to extract data:

```sql
-- Grid energy import (monthly)
SELECT 
  date_trunc('month', date) AS month,
  ROUND(SUM(total_kwh)::numeric, 0) AS grid_import_kwh
FROM energy_consumption_history 
WHERE site_id = 'site-002'
  AND date >= '2025-06-01' AND date <= '2026-05-31'
GROUP BY date_trunc('month', date)
ORDER BY month;

-- Solar generation (monthly)
SELECT 
  date_trunc('month', reading_time) AS month,
  ROUND(SUM(solar_yield_kwh)::numeric, 0) AS solar_kwh
FROM solar_bess 
WHERE site_id = 'site-002'
  AND reading_time >= '2025-06-01' AND reading_time <= '2026-05-31'
GROUP BY date_trunc('month', reading_time)
ORDER BY month;

-- BESS charge/discharge (monthly)
SELECT 
  date_trunc('month', reading_time) AS month,
  ROUND(SUM(charge_kwh)::numeric, 0) AS bess_charge_kwh,
  ROUND(SUM(discharge_kwh)::numeric, 0) AS bess_discharge_kwh
FROM solar_bess 
WHERE site_id = 'site-002'
  AND reading_time >= '2025-06-01' AND reading_time <= '2026-05-31'
GROUP BY date_trunc('month', reading_time)
ORDER BY month;

-- HVAC breakdown (monthly)
SELECT 
  date_trunc('month', date) AS month,
  ROUND(SUM(hvac_kwh)::numeric, 0) AS hvac_kwh,
  ROUND(SUM(lighting_kwh)::numeric, 0) AS lighting_kwh,
  ROUND(SUM(other_kwh)::numeric, 0) AS other_kwh
FROM energy_consumption_history 
WHERE site_id = 'site-002'
  AND date >= '2025-06-01' AND date <= '2026-05-31'
GROUP BY date_trunc('month', date)
ORDER BY month;
```

## 7. LEU Re-Assessment Schedule

Recalculate LEU status at the start of each calendar year and after any site expansion:

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Any single month >400,000 kWh | Flag for LEU registration; submit within 30 days |
| Combined entity >400,000 kWh/month | Aggregate all sites; register if total exceeds threshold |
| New building onboarding | Re-run LEU calculation immediately |
