# EU AI Act Readiness Mapping

## Timeline Anchors (as of 2026-02-23)

- Regulation entered into force: **2024-08-01**
- First obligations applied: **2025-02-02** (including prohibited practices and AI literacy obligations)
- Governance/GPAI-related provisions applied: **2025-08-02**
- General application date: **2026-08-02**
- Extended transition for specific product-embedded high-risk obligations: **2027-08-02**

## Current Readiness Snapshot

| Readiness Area | Current State | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| AI feature inventory and classification | Initial register created | Needs full feature coverage and legal sign-off |
| Prohibited practices control | Policy baseline drafted | Needs design-time and release-time checklist enforcement |
| AI literacy program | Requirement captured | Needs role-based curriculum and evidence register |
| User transparency | Partially implemented | Needs consistent channel-wide AI disclosure text |
| Incident handling | Existing IR process | Needs EU AI Act-specific trigger/escalation addendum |
| Technical traceability | Audit logs and decision trail exist | Needs packaged evidence chain by use case |
