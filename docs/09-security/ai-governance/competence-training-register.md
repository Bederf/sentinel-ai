# AI Competence and Training Register

## 1. Purpose

ISO/IEC 42001 (Clause 7.2) requires organisations to determine the necessary competence of persons doing work that affects AI system performance, ensure those persons are competent on the basis of appropriate education, training, or experience, and retain documented information as evidence of competence.

This register defines the competence requirements for each SENTINEL role, tracks training completion, and provides the auditable record needed for ISO 42001 conformity and EU AI Act Article 4 literacy compliance.

## 2. Role Competence Matrix

The following matrix defines the minimum AI literacy competence required for each role involved in SENTINEL AI system development, deployment, or operation.

| Role | Required Modules | Assessment Level | Refresh Cycle | Competence Justification |
|------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------|--------------------------|
| AI Engineering Lead | Module 1, Module 2, Module 3, Module 4 | Advanced (5/5 per module) | Annual | Designs, builds, and maintains AI features; must understand full pipeline, safety controls, approval workflow, and compliance obligations |
| Operations Lead | Module 1, Module 2, Module 3 | Intermediate (4/5 per module) | Annual | Approves Tier 2 recommendations; must understand AI capabilities, risk controls, and approval process |
| Facility Manager | Module 1, Module 2, Module 3 | Intermediate (4/5 per module) | Annual | Oversees building operations affected by AI recommendations; must evaluate and approve actions |
| Technician | Module 1, Module 2 | Basic (3/5 per module) | Annual | Receives and executes work orders generated from AI predictions; must understand AI fundamentals and safety controls |
| Compliance Officer | Module 1, Module 2, Module 3, Module 4 | Advanced (5/5 per module) | Annual | Audits AI governance controls; must understand full system, risk framework, and regulatory requirements |
| Security Lead | Module 2, Module 4 | Intermediate (4/5 per module) | Annual | Manages security aspects of AI systems; must understand safety controls and privacy/compliance obligations |

### 2.1 Assessment Level Definitions

| Level | Threshold | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Basic** | 3 out of 5 correct per required module | Demonstrates foundational understanding sufficient for supervised interaction with AI outputs |
| **Intermediate** | 4 out of 5 correct per required module | Demonstrates working knowledge sufficient for independent decision-making involving AI recommendations |
| **Advanced** | 5 out of 5 correct per required module | Demonstrates expert-level understanding sufficient for designing, auditing, or governing AI systems |

## 5. Non-Compliance Handling

If a person in an in-scope role has not completed required training by the target date:

1. **Escalation** -- The Compliance Officer notifies the person's line manager
2. **Restricted access** -- The person's ability to approve AI recommendations (Tier 2) is suspended until training is complete
3. **Remediation plan** -- A 14-day remediation window is provided with dedicated study time
4. **CAPA entry** -- Persistent non-compliance is logged as a nonconformity in the [`nonconformity-capa-register.md`](nonconformity-capa-register.md)
