Maintenance Module

Work Order Management, Scheduling & Technician Dispatch

Add-on

Overview

The Maintenance Module transforms reactive maintenance into a structured, tracked workflow. It generates work orders from AI recommendations and fault events, assigns them to technicians, tracks parts usage, and closes the loop with outcome verification. Every maintenance action is linked to the equipment that generated it, creating a complete maintenance history per asset.

Capabilities

  • Work order generation from AI recommendations, fault codes, and manual entry
  • Preventive maintenance scheduling — calendar-based and meter-based intervals
  • Technician assignment with skill-based routing (HVAC tech, electrical, DALI specialist)
  • Parts inventory tracking — links work orders to parts consumed for billing and reordering
  • Work order lifecycle: Open → Scheduled → In Progress → Resolved → Verified
  • Technician chat — in-app communication for clarification and updates
  • Attachment support — photos, meter readings, certificates attached to work orders
  • SLA tracking — alerts if work order is approaching or breaching its target resolution time

Work Order Triggers

Integration

Maintenance receives alerts from ML, HVAC, Fire, and Energy modules. Links to Assets for equipment history and Notifications for technician alerts. Outcome data feeds back to ML for model retraining and accuracy improvement.
