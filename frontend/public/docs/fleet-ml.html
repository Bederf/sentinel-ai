Fleet ML Module

Cross-Site Analytics, Portfolio Benchmarking & Fleet Intelligence

Add-on

Overview

The Fleet ML Module aggregates performance data across all buildings in the portfolio and uses cross-site ML models to identify efficiency opportunities that are only visible when comparing buildings to each other. It ranks sites by performance, surfaces best practices from top performers to the portfolio, and generates portfolio-level ESG reports.

Capabilities

  • Portfolio dashboard — KPI heatmap across all sites (energy intensity, water intensity, uptime)
  • Site ranking — ranked comparison of all buildings by efficiency, uptime, and AI adoption
  • Best practice transfer — identifies what top-performing sites do differently and recommends to laggers
  • Cross-site anomaly detection — flags a site behaving unusually relative to its peer group
  • Portfolio ESG reporting — aggregated carbon, energy, and water metrics for group-level disclosure
  • Multi-site energy baselining — each site's consumption normalized for weather and occupancy
  • Fleet-wide maintenance trending — tracks fault frequency and maintenance cost per equipment type across sites
  • Investment prioritization — ranks capital projects across the portfolio by projected ROI

AI Features

FeatureDescription
Peer benchmarkingML-adjusted comparison that accounts for building size, climate zone, and occupancy type

Fleet-wide failure correlationIdentifies if the same equipment model is failing across multiple sites → warranty/replacement alert
Project ROI rankingScores all capital projects across the fleet and recommends implementation sequence

Integration

Fleet ML reads data from all sites' Energy, HVAC, Solar, and Maintenance modules. Aggregated data is used for Compliance ESG reporting and Financial ROI analysis. Does not control any equipment — purely analytical.
