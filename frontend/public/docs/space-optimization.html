Space Optimization Module

Desk Utilization, Room Right-Sizing & Space Analytics

Add-on

Overview

The Space Optimization Module provides visibility into how the building's floor space is actually used versus how it was designed. Using occupancy sensors on desks, in meeting rooms, and at entry points, it generates heatmaps, utilization trends, and right-sizing recommendations — helping facilities managers make informed decisions about lease optimization, focus room conversion, and desking capacity.

Capabilities

  • Desk utilization heatmaps — live and historical occupancy color-coded per floor zone
  • Meeting room utilization tracking — actual vs. capacity usage per room per time slot
  • Entry/exit flow analysis — peak entry times, floor distribution, queueing detection
  • Right-sizing recommendations — flags over-sized or under-used rooms that could be converted
  • Hot-desk management — real-time availability map for unassigned seating areas
  • Focus room utilization — tracks quiet rooms, phone booths, and collaboration zones separately
  • Space occupancy reporting — daily/weekly/monthly utilization reports per floor and per zone
  • Tenant density reporting — m² per person vs. lease commitment for tenant charge-back

Integration

Space Optimization reads occupancy data from HVAC zone sensors and standalone desk sensors. Feeds into Block Booking for ghost desk detection. Uses Lighting occupancy signals as a secondary source for validation. Feeds utilization reports to Financial and Fleet ML.
