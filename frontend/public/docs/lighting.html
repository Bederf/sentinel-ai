Lighting Module

DALI-2 Lighting Control & Illuminance Management

Building System

Overview

The Lighting Module manages all artificial lighting across a building via DALI-2 (Digital Addressable Lighting Interface). It monitors zone-level illuminance via photocell sensors, controls ballast brightness, activates scene presets, and integrates with occupancy sensors for automatic on/off and dim-to-background lighting. Lighting significantly impacts HVAC load — the module coordinates with HVAC to optimize total energy consumption.

Capabilities

  • DALI-2 bus management — up to 64 DALI devices per controller, multiple controllers per floor
  • Brightness control (0–100%) per luminaire or zone group
  • Scene presets — configurable lighting scenes per zone (Presentation, After Hours, Night, Emergency)
  • Occupancy-based control — auto-on at arrival, dim to 20% after 15 min vacancy, auto-off after 30 min
  • Photocell daylight harvesting — dims luminaires when natural light is sufficient
  • Emergency lighting test scheduling — automated functional and duration testing
  • Energy consumption tracking per floor/zone in kWh
  • Lux level monitoring per zone — ensures minimum illuminance compliance

Integration

Lighting reads from DALI-2 via Tridonic or Helvar gateway. Control requires Lighting Control add-on. Integrates with HVAC (occupancy signals), Energy (kWh tracking), and Space Optimization (zone utilization data).
