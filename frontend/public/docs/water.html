Water Module

Water Consumption Monitoring, Leak Detection & Conservation

Building System

Overview

The Water Module monitors municipal water consumption across all building zones using pulse-output or Modbus water meters. It tracks consumption in kL, detects abnormal flow patterns that indicate leaks, and provides trend analysis to identify water waste. This is especially critical for large multi-tenant buildings where tenant charge-backs require accurate submetering.

Capabilities

  • Zone-level water metering — main riser, floor, and key equipment water circuits
  • Real-time flow rate (L/min) and cumulative consumption (kL) tracking
  • Leak detection via continuous flow analysis — alerts on 24/7 flow when building is unoccupied
  • Submeter integration for tenant billing and charge-back calculation
  • Rainwater harvesting and greywater reuse system monitoring (if installed)
  • Consumption benchmarking against historical baseline and peer buildings
  • Automated daily/weekly consumption reports by zone

Integration

Water reads from Modbus or 4–20 mA transmitters. Control requires Water Control add-on (valve shutoff). Feeds KPI and Sustainability Reports. Works with Maintenance for work order generation on leak detection.
