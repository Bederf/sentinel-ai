HVAC Module

Heating, Ventilation & Air Conditioning — Monitoring & Control

Building System

Overview

The HVAC Module manages all air handling and temperature control equipment — Air Handling Units (AHUs), Variable Air Volume boxes (VAVs), Fan Coil Units (FCUs), chillers, and boilers. It provides real-time monitoring of temperature, humidity, fan status, and valve positions, and exposes control points for setpoint adjustments, fan speed changes, and reheat valve modulation.

Equipment Types

  • AHU (Air Handling Unit) — central air handling with supply/return fans, heating/cooling coils, filters, and mixing boxes. Manages primary air delivery to multiple VAVs.
  • VAV (Variable Air Volume) — zone-level air distribution box with motorized damper. Controls airflow to maintain zone temperature setpoints.
  • FCU (Fan Coil Unit) — compact unit serving a single zone (typically per floor section). Has a fan, coil, and local temperature sensor.
  • Chiller / Boiler — central plant providing chilled water or heated water to the AHUs.
  • Heat Pump — rooftop or central heat pump for supplementary heating/cooling.

Integration

HVAC reads from BACnet/IP (Siemens Desigo, JCI Niagara) via SIMBIOT. Control requires HVAC Control add-on to be enabled. HVAC data feeds KPI, ML, and Digital Twin. Works with DALI Lighting for occupant comfort correlation (lights + HVAC together).
