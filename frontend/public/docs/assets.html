Assets Module

Equipment Registry & Asset Lifecycle Management

Platform

Overview

The Assets Module is the master registry of all equipment in a building — HVAC units, lighting controllers, power meters, fire panels, and more. It maintains the canonical identity of every device, its hierarchy (AHU → VAV → FCU → Zone), location metadata, and lifecycle state. The registry feeds every other module — without it, no equipment can be addressed or controlled.

Capabilities

  • Equipment registry with unique IDs, types, names, and site locations
  • Hierarchical relationships — chiller → AHU → VAV → FCU → zone are linked
  • Equipment discovery — SIMBIOT auto-populates the registry on initial connection
  • Manual override and bulk import for known equipment not auto-discovered
  • Lifecycle state tracking — Commissioned, Active, Maintenance, Decommissioned, Fault
  • Equipment metadata — manufacturer, model, serial number, installation date, firmware version
  • Warranty and maintenance contract expiry tracking with automated alerts
  • Equipment document attachments — manuals, certificates, inspection reports
  • Brick schema ontology mapping — each asset is tagged with a Brick class for Digital Twin
