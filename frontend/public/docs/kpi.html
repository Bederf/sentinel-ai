KPI Module

Key Performance Indicators — Real-time Metric Dashboard

Platform

Overview

The KPI Module delivers real-time and historical performance metrics across all building systems. It aggregates data from HVAC, energy, lighting, solar, water, and fire systems into a unified dashboard, enabling facilities managers to monitor efficiency, identify anomalies, and track savings against targets.

Capabilities

  • Live dashboard with configurable KPI cards (energy consumption, water usage, comfort indices)
  • Historical trend charts with configurable time windows (1h, 24h, 7d, 30d, 12m)
  • Benchmarking against similar buildings in the fleet
  • Threshold alerting — automatic notification when a metric exceeds defined limits
  • Export metrics as CSV or PDF for reporting periods
  • Cross-module correlation (e.g., energy vs. occupancy, HVAC load vs. IAQ)
  • Target vs. actual tracking with variance analysis

Integration Points

KPI is the aggregation layer — it reads from Energy, HVAC, Solar, Water, and Lighting modules. It does not control equipment directly; its output feeds into the AI Optimization and Fleet ML modules for cross-system analysis.
