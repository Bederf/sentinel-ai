Solar & BESS Module

Solar Generation & Battery Energy Storage System Monitoring

Building System

Overview

The Solar & BESS Module monitors photovoltaic (PV) generation and battery energy storage systems, tracks self-consumption versus export, and manages battery charge/discharge cycles to maximize solar utilization and reduce grid dependence. It integrates with Huawei and Sungrow inverters and supports BESS systems from multiple OEMs.

Capabilities

  • Real-time solar generation monitoring per string/inverter (kW, kWh)
  • Battery state-of-charge (SoC) tracking, charge/discharge power (kW), cycle count
  • Self-consumption optimization — prioritizes on-site solar use over grid export
  • BESS discharge scheduling — draws from battery during peak tariff windows
  • Grid import/export tracking per 5-minute interval with cumulative kWh
  • Inverter efficiency monitoring — flags underperforming strings or inverters
  • Annual performance ratio (PR) calculation for solar fleet benchmarking
  • Weather-adjusted generation forecasts for next-day energy planning

Integration

Solar & BESS reads from Modbus TCP inverters and BMS. Control requires Solar Control add-on. Feeds Energy, KPI, and AI Optimization. Works with Battery Arbitrage Engine for automated discharge scheduling.
