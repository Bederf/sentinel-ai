Compliance Module

Regulatory Compliance, ESG Reporting & Audit Readiness

Add-on

Overview

The Compliance Module tracks regulatory obligations across South African and international standards (POPIA, SANS 10400, ISO 50001, Green Star), maintains evidence of compliance, and generates audit-ready reports. It automates recurring compliance tasks — fire equipment inspections, elevator certifications, IAQ measurements — so nothing lapses. ESG metrics (energy intensity, water intensity, carbon emissions) are calculated and reported for Green Star and BREEAM certification.

Capabilities

  • Regulatory compliance calendar — inspections, certifications, and renewals tracked with automated alerts
  • Compliance evidence repository — upload and link certificates, test reports, and audit letters
  • ISO 50001 energy management — document energy review, action plan, and performance tracking
  • Green Star / BREEAM ESG reporting — energy intensity (kWh/m²), water intensity (kL/m²), carbon (kgCO₂/m²)
  • Fire equipment inspection tracking — extinguishers, hoses, sprinkler pumps with expiry alerts
  • POPIA data processing register — maintain records of all personal data processing activities
  • Audit workflow — pre-defined audit checklists for internal and external audit preparation
  • Non-conformance tracking — CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Action) register for identified gaps
