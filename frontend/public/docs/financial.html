Financial Module

Contracts, Billing, Cost Allocation & ROI Tracking

Add-on

Overview

The Financial Module tracks operational expenditure linked to energy, water, and maintenance contracts. It manages supplier contracts (energy supply, maintenance contracts, warranty agreements), allocates costs to tenants based on submeter data, and provides ROI analysis for energy efficiency projects and maintenance investments.

Capabilities

  • Supplier contract management — energy supply, maintenance, warranty agreements with expiry alerts
  • Energy cost budgeting — set annual/monthly energy spend targets per building or tenant
  • Tenant charge-back calculation — allocate energy, water, and HVAC costs to tenant billing periods
  • Municipal bill verification — cross-check utility bills against SENTINEL metered consumption
  • ROI tracking for capital projects — energy savings, maintenance cost avoidance, downtime reduction
  • Invoice matching — automated matching of supplier invoices against contracted rates
  • Contract escalation management — fixed-price vs. variable-rate tracking and anomaly alerts

Integration

Financial Module reads consumption data from Energy, Water, and Solar. Links maintenance costs from Maintenance module. Tenant data comes from Contracts add-on. Feeds reports to KPI and executive dashboards.
