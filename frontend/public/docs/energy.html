Energy Module

Electrical Power Monitoring, Cost Tracking & Optimization

Building System

Overview

The Energy Module monitors electrical consumption across all circuits, tracks energy costs against tariff structures, and identifies waste or inefficiency. It ingests data from power meters (Schneider PM8000, Eaton 93PM, ABB M4M) and correlates consumption with HVAC, lighting, and solar generation to produce accurate per-circuit and per-zone energy balances.

Capabilities

  • Real-time kW and kWh metering per circuit via Modbus TCP power meters
  • Time-of-use (TOU) tariff tracking — separates peak, standard, and off-peak consumption
  • Demand charge management — tracks kVA demand peaks, forecasts demand spikes
  • Power factor monitoring — alerts when PF drops below 0.95 (utility penalty threshold)
  • Energy balance verification — solar generation + grid import = building consumption ± 2%
  • Load disaggregation — separates base building load from tenant load
  • Automated energy report generation with per-circuit and per-zone breakdowns
  • Carbon intensity tracking — converts kWh to kgCO₂ using municipal grid factor

Integration

Energy reads from Modbus TCP power meters and BACnet grid meters. Feeds KPI, Solar, BESS, and AI Optimization. Requires Energy Control add-on for active load management (demand response, peak shaving).
