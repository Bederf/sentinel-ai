Sustainability Module

ESG Metrics, Carbon Tracking & Sustainability Reporting

Add-on

Overview

The Sustainability Module calculates and tracks environmental impact metrics across energy, water, waste, and transport — providing the data foundation for Green Star, BREEAM, and CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive) submissions. It replaces manual spreadsheet-based ESG tracking with automated, audit-ready sustainability reporting.

Capabilities

  • Carbon footprint tracking — Scope 1 (gas boilers), Scope 2 (electricity), Scope 3 (tenant travel, water, waste)
  • Energy intensity (kWh/m²/year), water intensity (kL/m²/year), waste intensity (kg/m²/year) reporting
  • Green Star submission data — energy, water, transport, indoor environment category evidence
  • Renewable energy fraction — % of total consumption met by solar generation
  • Water recycling tracking — rainwater harvesting and greywater reuse volumes
  • Embodied carbon estimation for major equipment replacements
  • Net Zero pathway tracking — gap analysis against science-based targets
  • Tenant sustainability scorecards — shared data for tenant ESG reporting requirements

ScopeEmission SourceCalculation Method

Scope 3Water supply (embodied), wastewater treatment, waste to landfill, business travelkL × water factor, kg waste × landfill factor, km × vehicle factor

Integration

Sustainability reads from Energy (electricity, gas), Water (municipal consumption), Solar (renewable generation), and Fleet ML (cross-site aggregation). Outputs to Compliance (ESG reports) and Financial (carbon tax exposure). Requires Compliance add-on for regulatory reporting.
