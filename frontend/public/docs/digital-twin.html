Digital Twin Module

3D Building Visualization, Asset Relationships & Spatial Intelligence

Building System

Overview

The Digital Twin Module provides a 3D visual representation of the building, mapping every monitored asset to its physical location and linking it to its equipment hierarchy and live telemetry. It creates a living digital replica that operators and AI systems can navigate spatially to understand conditions at a glance, investigate anomalies in context, and simulate the impact of control actions before they are applied.

Capabilities

  • 3D floor plan viewer with equipment icons positioned by zone/room
  • Live telemetry overlay — color-coded equipment status on the floor plan
  • Equipment hierarchy browser — drill from building → floor → zone → equipment → point
  • Brick schema ontology — all assets tagged with Brick classes for standardized queries
  • DXF floor plan import — converts architect CAD files to interactive floor plan views
  • Equipment detail panel — click any asset to see live readings, recent events, and history
  • What-if simulation — preview the effect of setpoint changes before applying them
  • Maintenance overlay — highlights assets due for inspection with work order links
  • Fire zone overlay — visualizes fire zone boundaries and detector locations

Integration

Digital Twin consumes live data from all building system modules. It is the primary visualization layer for HVAC, Energy, Lighting, Fire, and Water. Requires DXF upload to initialize the floor plan. Feeds into AI Recommendations by providing spatial context for anomalies.
